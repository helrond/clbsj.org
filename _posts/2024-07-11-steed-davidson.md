Our second session will feature Dr. Steed Vernyl Davidson, Hebrew Bible scholar and Executive Director of the Society of Biblical Literature, from Trinidad and Tobago. 
Working with Jeremiah 38:7-13, Dr. Davidson will explore how Ethiopianism opened the space for the idealization of African and Africans in the Bible, as well as offering support for liberationist movements in the late 19th century into the 20th century.

Dr. Davidson focuses on the consequences of empire on the shape and reading of the Bible, using postcolonial and other critical theories to read and teach the Bible in diverse settings. His internationalist scholarship draws from his experience as a native of Trinidad and Tobago, educated in Jamaica, Boston and New York.
