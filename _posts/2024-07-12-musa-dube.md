Our fourth Black August session will feature Musa Wenkosi Dube Shomanah, Botswanan biblical scholar and theologian and former President of the Society of Biblical Literature. Dr. Dube will discuss the call she delivered to the field of biblical scholarship in November 2023 to direct scholarly attention toward researching and understanding the process of Biblical translation into the many languages around the world.
