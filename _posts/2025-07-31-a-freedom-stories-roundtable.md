How does the ideal of Freedom — from a Christian, Panafricanist and Abolitionist perspective — relate to the process of struggling for liberation, both within our own lives and bodies, and within our social structures? What guidance can scripture offer to us as we seek to discern, comprehend and walk this path? This Black August, CLBSJ invites all seekers of freedom to gather on Saturday, August 30 for a freedom stories roundtable. Drawing from the knowledge contained in Black experience, the testimonies of Black ancestors, and the wisdom of our scriptural traditions, we will explore how our experiences of struggle and overcoming reflect and refract biblical understandings of freedom. Featured speakers will include New Testament Scholar Mitzi J. Smith — who will share based on her new book, _Not Wanting a Thing to Be the Thing: An African American Woman Biblical Scholar’s Stroke Memoir_ — and others to be announced. People of all faiths and backgrounds are invited to join in this participatory gathering.
