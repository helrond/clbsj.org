CLBSJ is excited to welcome Terry Wildman for a sharing and bible study with the _First Nations Version of Psalms and Proverbs_, recently published by InterVarsity Press. Building upon the well received _FNV: An Indigenous Translation of the New Testament_, Wildman's team has crafted a dynamic equivalence translation of the beloved books Psalms and Proverbs, drawing from the cultural and linguistic thought patterns found in the tongues and traditions of First Nations peoples. In this session, Wildman will share about the history, motivations and process of creating this unique translation. We will then delve into a few scriptures that speak strongly to our current times.
