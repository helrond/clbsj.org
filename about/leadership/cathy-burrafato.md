Cathy brings decades of experience in supporting faith-based and justice-oriented organizations including as the Resource Center Coordinator for the Newton Presbytery Regional Office, the live-in Caretaker of Stony Point Center’s Gilmor Sloan House, and the Administrative Secretary for First Memorial Presbyterian Church. Cathy also has worked as a spiritual director and a Christian educator, and a media entrepreneur through a family-owned newspaper and graphic design business.
