Eric A. Thomas is an Assistant Professor of Bible at the General
Theological Seminary of the Episcopal Church. His research interests
include Queer biblical interpretation, Diasporic Studies and Homiletics.
Eric is a graduate of Drew University where he completed the Ph.D. in
New Testament with concentrations in Africana, and Women and Gender
Studies, and the Interdenominational Theological Center where he
completed his M.Div. His research explores the potential for queer
people of color to engage with scriptural texts to articulate and embody
their own modes of flourishing. In addition to his scholarship, Eric is
a pastor and an activist, having served since 2017 at the Siloam
Presbyterian Church in Bedford Stuyvesant, Brooklyn.
