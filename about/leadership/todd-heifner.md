Todd is the Executive Director of Hope Manifest and a longtime advisor to faith-based justice initiatives. For three decades, he has been helping organizations become more successful through campaign planning, fundraising, donor database evaluation and selection, capital campaign development, and management consulting. He holds an M.Ed. with a concentration in Institutional Advancement from Peabody College at Vanderbilt University, an M.B.A. from the Brock School of Business at Samford University, and a B.S. in Public Administration from Samford. He has also completed coursework toward the Ph.D. in Political Science at the University of Alabama.

Before co-founding Hope Manifest, Todd consulted in major donor development, foundations, and capital funds with organizations such as Samford University, the Baptist Joint Committee, Associated Baptist Press, Cumberland University, the Woman’s Missionary Union, United Way, the Joint Campaign Against Homelessness in Birmingham, among others. 

He is married to Amanda Hiley. Together they have five sons, Graham and Deason Heifner, Jess, Andy, and Nathan Vaughan. They also have a grandson, Julian Heifner.
