A jazz musician and artistic entrepreneur from Philadelphia, Warren works with CLBSJ on audio visual production and media strategy development. He is the Executive Producer of Music Media Ministry, a multi-media production company focused on the manufacturing and trafficking of positive art that nurtures the movement for justice and peace, and for years hosted and produced the gospel music program, Ovations, on WRTI. Warren is also an Ordained Ruling Elder and Minister of Music in the Presbyterian Church USA, and an activist for justice and peace through many initiatives including the Community of Living Traditions and the Interfaith Peace Walk. Learn more about Warren's work at [https://warrencoopervoice.com](https://warrencoopervoice.com)
