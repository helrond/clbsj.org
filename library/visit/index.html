CLBSJ's collection is available for use by scholars and activists from all walks of life, regardless of institutional or religious affiliation. Due to limited capacity, and because we want our materials to be available at any time for research, we do not currently lend out books. The collection can be consulted on site only.

Visits by Appointment

First-time users can access our collection by making an appointment. Please reach out to us via phone or email: 845-603-2508 or [info@clbsj.org](mailto:info@clbsj.org)

Parking

There is free parking in the parking lot adjacent to the library and in the lot adjacent to the baseball field below.

Accessibility

The Library is handicapped accessible. Please inform us prior to arriving if special assistance will be required.
